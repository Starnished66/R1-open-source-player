partial swap from the same day -- this is the full, current mapping)
Target sizes: 28x30 (status bar glyphs), 20x30 (battery indicators), 48x30
(audio codec format badges). Digits (0-9.png, colon.png, percent.png) are a
separate typography pass, documented at the end of this file.

Numeric Readouts Typography: Barlow Condensed (Bold)
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL)
Author: Jeremy Tribby
https://github.com/jpt/barlow
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Barlow+Condensed
Selected over Current Stock, Montserrat Bold, Roboto Condensed Bold, Antonio
Bold, and IBM Plex Sans Condensed Bold for legibility at actual render size:
23px glyph height within the 30px canvas (vs 16-20px for the alternatives),
sturdy 3.5-4.0px stems, and open counters that stay distinct at tiny scale.
Comparison renders: topbar/icons/typography_comparison.png and
typography_comparison_10x.png (not copied into the live theme).

Numeric Mapping:
  0.png..9.png <- Barlow Condensed Bold '0'..'9' #FFFFFF (14x30)
  colon.png <- Barlow Condensed Bold ':' #FFFFFF (14x30)
  percent.png <- Barlow Condensed Bold '%' #FFFFFF (18x30)
